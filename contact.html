Frequently Asked Questions

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary depending on the visa type and destination country. Typically, work visas take 3-8 weeks, study visas 4-10 weeks, and visit visas 1-2 weeks. We provide a specific timeline after initial consultation.

What documents do I need for a work visa?

Required documents typically include: passport, job offer letter, educational certificates, resume, birth certificate, medical clearance, and police clearance. We provide a complete checklist during consultation.

What are your service fees?

Our fees vary based on the service and destination. We offer transparent, competitive pricing with no hidden charges. Contact us for a detailed quote tailored to your specific needs.

Can I track my application status?

Yes! All our clients have access to their online portal where they can track application progress in real-time. We also provide regular email and SMS updates.

What if my visa application is rejected?

If an application is rejected, we provide free consultation to understand the reason and help you reapply with a stronger application. Our success rate is 98%.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for most services. You can pay 50% upfront and the balance upon visa approval. Ask our team about available options.
