About Biggy Fly Agency

Your trusted partner in global mobility and career development

Who We Are

Biggy Fly Agency is a premier immigration and travel services company based in Kenya. With offices in Nairobi and Meru, we've been helping thousands of Africans achieve their dreams of working, studying, and traveling globally.

Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to providing seamless visa processing, job placement, and travel services to clients seeking opportunities in the Middle East and Europe.
